# TT-05 — Round robin and scheduling

Status: Independent QA PASS; open awaiting explicit authorization for orchestrator closure
GitHub issue: https://github.com/mjesenuly/tournament-tracker/issues/5
Product source: ../plan.md

## Goal

Generate every accepted team pairing exactly once and let owner schedule fixtures.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At least two accepted teams are required; pair count is n(n−1)/2.
- [ ] Odd/even team tests show no self-pairing, duplicate pair or missing pair.
- [ ] Repeated generation cannot duplicate fixtures; team admissions lock after generation.
- [ ] Owner edits date/time/venue and spectators see schedules; unauthorized edits fail.

## Out of scope

Automatic venue allocation and extra rounds.

## Constraints

Document timezone and scheduling conflict policy; no unpublished rest-period claims. Depends on 04.

## Handoff and verification

Engineer records actual implementation commit and executed checks. Independent QA records criterion evidence and PASS/FAIL; failures return to engineer. Orchestrator alone closes a real issue after QA PASS. No implementation or publication is claimed by this specification.
